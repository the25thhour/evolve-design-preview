Aldo Balatti

D.I.D., IDSA, SPE, SME

Principal & Founder Senior Product Designer / Plastic Applications Specialist

Aldo founded Evolve Design Solutions Inc. in 2005, after 15 years as a consultant in product development at two major design firms, where he led the plastic applications department and held the title of Design Engineering Director.

His experience covers 3D CAD modelling, mold filling analysis and simulation, injection molded components and plastic materials selection. Aldo has worked with Black & Decker, Nortel Networks, Motorola, General Motors, Electrolux, Canada Post, MDS Nordion, Hydro-Quebec, C-MAC and CCM.

Selected work

C-POD DNA Sample Collection Container

Challenge
A smart DNA sample collection container to preserve genetic material such as buccal cell, tissue and blood or liquid spot prior to laboratory testing.
Evolve’s role
Design and engineering development collaboration by Evolve Design Solutions. The C-POD is moulded flat as a single piece: a buccal collector, a bottom housing and a top housing, linked together by folding break-away tabs.
Result
Announced as patent pending in March 2012.

Slim Asymmetric Twister Armrest

Client
One of North America’s leading manufacturers of office seating
Challenge
A slim asymmetric armrest that pivots fully: it rotates 360 degrees and can stop or lock in any position.
Evolve’s role
Design and engineering. The armrest and its mechanism are made of structural die-cast zinc and polyurethane foam components; structural FEA and 3D rapid prototyping validated the final design.
Result
Launched at NeoCon 2008 in Chicago.

Resolute GNSS Receiver

Client
Xeos Technologies
Challenge
A new autonomous reference receiver: low power, small form factor and user configurable.
Evolve’s role
The conceptual design exploration phase. Evolve created and presented multiple design concepts.
Result
One concept was selected for Xeos Technologies’ engineering team to engineer and implement for production.

About Evolve

Evolve is a product design consultancy with manufacturing and plastics expertise. We take a product from first research through engineering and tooling to its production launch, so the design intent survives into the finished product.

Aldo Balatti, D.I.D. Industrial Design Diploma, Georgian College, 1984. Fifteen years at two design firms, where he led the plastic applications department as Design Engineering Director, then founded Evolve in 2005.

Plastics first. Over 700 injection-molded parts engineered. Material and process selection, mold filling, warping and cooling analysis and tooling reviews, with computer-aided engineering that reduces prototyping, tooling and testing costs.

Many industries. Electronics, appliances, telecom, consumer, medical, sporting goods and industrial products. Aldo has worked with Black & Decker, Nortel Networks, Motorola, General Motors, Electrolux, Canada Post, MDS Nordion, Hydro-Quebec, C-MAC and CCM.

How we work. A proven, phased development process, on a fee-for-service proposal or a design services retainer, drawing on strategic partners and a database of domestic and worldwide tooling and production vendors.

The 4D process

  1. Discover new opportunities

    Research the market, the users, the competition and existing patents, then set the target costs and the critical design goals.

  2. Define the idea

    Explore concepts and usability, lay out the engineering, study feasibility, and set specifications and preliminary tooling costs.

  3. Design and validate

    Finalize the form, build the 3D CAD and production drawings, qualify suppliers and prove form, fit and function with working models.

  4. Deliver the launch

    Run FEA and mold filling analysis, finalize the bill of materials, manage engineering changes and support the production launch.

What we do

  • Project management

    Clear expectations, schedules and regular progress updates, for small and Fortune 500 companies alike.

  • Research

    Market and user understanding that turns field data into a strategy for the product.

  • Industrial design

    Concepts, form and design language that add functional, emotional, cultural and social value.

  • Mechanical design

    Mechanisms and electro-mechanical solutions, from quick proofs of concept to long programs.

  • Product engineering

    Engineering from the start of a project, so the design meets its function and its manufacturing.

  • Plastics engineering

    Over 700 injection-molded parts engineered. Part design, materials, mold filling and tooling.

  • Prototyping

    Mock-ups, models and engineering prototypes that prove form, fit and function before release.

  • Manufacturing support

    Sourcing, tooling supervision and launch support that keep the design intent through production.

Capabilities

We believe a great product is an accomplishment, achieved through strategic partnerships with clients and suppliers.

Each project starts with your vision and the design challenges you face. We bring creative thinking, technical expertise, the ability to combine form with function, and the resources of strategic partners who are as committed to your product as we are.

Eight disciplines, one team

Project management

A design program starts with planning and due diligence. We define expectations and responsibilities, set communication procedures and schedules, and ask the questions that make the difference between a delay and a job done on time.

You get progress updates at regular intervals, so you know the status of the project at any time. Our methodology tracks milestones to keep the work on task and on budget, for small companies and Fortune 500 companies alike.

  • Planning and initial due diligence
  • Responsibilities and expectations
  • Communication procedures and schedules
  • Milestone and progress tracking
  • Regular progress updates

Research

Information is the start of good design. It defines the problem, finds the opportunity and sets the objectives and design criteria for the design and manufacturing phases.

Our research builds a market and user-centred understanding of your business, and finds what makes a product more appealing to the end user or the buyer: features, ergonomics, aesthetics, colour, graphics or packaging. We turn raw field data into a strategy for product innovation.

  • Market and user research
  • Competitive analysis
  • Patent review
  • Design criteria and objectives
  • Field data into product strategy

Industrial design

By finding and strengthening the functional, emotional, cultural and social benefits of a product, we raise its value to the person who uses it.

We observe and brainstorm, develop functional concepts, suggest new features and better materials, and create design languages that encourage ownership and build strong brands, for challenging products and for the simplest ones.

  • Conceptual development
  • Ergonomic evaluation
  • Product semantics exploration
  • Product styling
  • Appearance and design language development
  • Form study mock-ups and models
  • Colour and form studies
  • Product graphics

Mechanical design

Mechanical design for every stage of development, complementing your own team’s strengths: from quick-turnaround proof-of-concept projects to sustained innovation programs.

Our mechanical design and engineering resources bring experience across many industries and applications.

  • Simple to complex mechanism development
  • Electro-mechanical development solutions
  • 3D CAD virtual mechanical prototyping
  • Physical design intent prototypes
  • Mechanical components sourcing
  • Reverse engineering

Product engineering

Engineering expertise and manufacturing know-how set Evolve apart from other design firms. Our multidisciplinary approach keeps the design intent of the original concept intact in the final product.

Whether the project is a new technology or a new design around an existing platform, engineering is involved from the start, so the design meets its functional requirements and its manufacturing needs. Our team works closely with designers, clients and manufacturing partners.

  • Materials, finishes and processes (plastics and metal)
  • Plastics, sheet metal, die casting, extrusion, stamping
  • Tolerance and dimensional analysis
  • DFMA (design for manufacture and assembly)
  • 3D CAD solid and surface modelling
  • 2D CAD control drawings and documentation
  • Structural FEA, thermal and CFD analysis
  • Regulatory compliance
  • EMI/RFI and ESD solutions
  • Performance, environmental, quality and safety compliance

Plastics engineering

A successful plastic product needs part design optimization, material selection, feasibility, cost evaluation, mold design, process selection and prototyping. Our plastics experience covers a wide variety of end-use applications. We have expertise in injection molding, part design, plastic materials selection, insert molding, gas assist, assembly techniques, and painting and decorating.

Our computer-aided engineering (CAE) makes sure your parts are designed for the resins and processes you choose, which reduces prototyping, tooling and physical testing costs.

  • Injection-molded part engineering and development
  • Materials and processes review and selection
  • Mold filling, warping and cooling analysis
  • Impact and cyclic non-linear FEA
  • Part and tooling cost quotation and evaluation
  • Domestic and offshore vendor sourcing and qualification
  • Tooling design and build review and supervision
  • Thermoforming, extrusion, blow molding and rotomolding assistance

Prototyping

Mock-ups, volumetric and visual models and engineering validation prototypes prove out form, fit, function and user acceptance before engineering is finalized and the design is released to manufacturing.

Prototypes also support marketing and sales while production ramps up.

  • Mock-ups, volumetric, visual and functional models
  • MJM, SLA, SLS and FDM rapid prototypes
  • Urethane, epoxy and polyester casting
  • Metal forming and fabrication
  • Plastic and metal CNC machining
  • Thermoforming
  • Finishing, painting, decorating and graphics
  • Assembly and wiring of functional prototypes
  • Prototype build management
  • Small volume production runs

Manufacturing support

We bring manufacturing insight to specifications, materials and design for manufacturing, so the design intent is preserved through the production launch. We also advise your manufacturing and management teams on supply chain, inventory and production questions.

Our vendor database covers domestic and worldwide tooling and production manufacturers. We can draw on it, or find and qualify new vendors for your project, then set expectations, responsibilities and schedules through production ramp-up.

  • Part and tooling cost quotation and evaluation
  • Domestic and offshore vendor sourcing and qualification
  • Tooling design and build review and supervision
  • First article inspection and ECO management
  • In-progress quality inspection and testing assistance
  • Pilot production and rollout supervision

The 4D process

Four stages that take an idea to a successful launch, with less time, less risk and less cost: we discover, define, design and deliver.

  1. Discover

    Research new opportunities

    • Collect project information, data and components
    • Review competitive analysis
    • Review existing patents
    • Attend user focus groups or review their data
    • Identify new technology, market trends and customer needs
    • Review objectives, mechanical requirements, target costs and timeline
    • Establish the project’s critical design goals

  2. Define

    Explore and define new ideas

    • Explore concepts, aesthetics and usability
    • Product engineering layouts
    • Feasibility studies and report
    • Establish product specifications
    • Research technologies, components and suppliers
    • Define preliminary component and tooling costs
    • Explore product graphics, branding and user interface

  3. Design

    Prove out and validate concepts

    • Finalize product aesthetics
    • Identify and qualify suppliers
    • Preliminary 3D CAD parts and assemblies
    • Preliminary 2D production documentation
    • Performance, quality and test plan specifications
    • Product standards and certifications to meet
    • Validate form, fit, function and design intent
    • Validate and test functional models
    • Refine product graphics and branding

  4. Deliver

    Ensure a successful product launch

    • Finalize aesthetics, colours, graphics and finishes
    • Design, quality and safety reviews
    • Final 3D CAD parts and assemblies
    • Final 2D production documentation
    • FEA and mold filling analysis
    • Support the production launch
    • Evaluate manufacturing requests
    • Assist in product testing
    • Implement engineering change orders (ECOs)
    • Finalize the bill of materials (BOM)
    • Benchmark and refine the production process

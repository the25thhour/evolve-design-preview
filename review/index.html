Notes for Aldo

This is a design preview of a refreshed Evolve website. Everything on it comes from your current site, rewritten for clarity.

A few things only you can answer or supply. Each one is below, in plain terms. The preview is hidden from search engines, and this page and the bar at the top of every page go away at launch.

What we need from you

  1. Larger images

    Every portfolio image on the current site is about 500 pixels wide, the largest size the server holds. Larger renders or photographs (2000 pixels wide or more) would let the work be shown at full size. Most useful first: a single clean render, on white, for each project now shown as a collage or a photograph.

  2. A vector logo

    Only a small image of the logo exists (187 by 60 pixels), so it looks soft on sharp screens. An SVG, EPS or PDF of the current mark would fix that, and would give the site a proper browser icon (the preview uses a plain red “e” for now). The mark itself stays exactly as it is.

  3. A recent portrait

    The current portrait is 192 by 280 pixels. A newer, larger photograph would let it be shown properly on the About and Contact pages.

  4. A description for the SkyCase

    On the current site, the Man-Portable SkyCase System page shows the text of the Amisco Freestyle stools by mistake, so the preview shows its picture with no description. A line or two about what it is and what Evolve did would complete it. Is it the man-portable energy system battery pack from your April 2012 news item (for solar and wind power, tested from -10ºC to +50ºC)? If so, we can use that text.

Questions about the work

  1. Is this the Unitron Smart Control remote?

    The current site lists two projects called “Smart Alert Remote Control Alerting System”. The second one’s picture shows a remote printed “smart control”, and its text describes a compact wireless remote for hearing instruments. The preview calls it Smart Control Remote, with no client. Is it the Unitron smart remote control featured in DPN (November 2008) and Design World (March 2009)? If so, we will name Unitron and mention both articles.

  2. Your work before Evolve

    11 projects are credited to you at GID Design or DW, before you founded Evolve. The preview keeps them, marked with a grey tag (“Aldo Balatti at GID Design”, “Aldo Balatti at DW”) and listed separately on About. Do you want them shown, and are those labels right? What is DW’s full name? The Workmate 375 credit also names Camalor Mfg.: should that stay?

  3. Collaborations and concept work

    6 Evolve projects are credited as collaborations or concept work (Resolute GNSS Receiver, C-POD DNA Sample Collection Container, Classroom Audio Pod Listening Centre, Classroom Teaching Easel Station, Small Back-to-Back Tabletop Easel, Concierge Shopping Cart Handle). They are grouped with Evolve, and each page shows its exact credit. Is that how you want them presented?

  4. The nine sectors

    The current site has no categories, so the preview groups the work into nine sectors of our own (hearing instruments, power and batteries, and so on). Would you name or group any of them differently?

  5. “Evolve Italla”

    The MITO office seating title includes “Evolve Italla”, which the current site does not explain. What does it refer to, and should it stay in the title?

  6. Process images

    The sketches, marker rendering, exploded view, stress plot, mold filling simulation and molded part from your About and Services pages now appear on the home page as examples of the process, each captioned with the 4D stage it illustrates. Are you happy for them to be shown that way, and are the stages right? Two others need a word from you: the close-up of a round grey part on About (what is it?), and the concept renders of three portable devices at the top of Capabilities (which project, and whose work?).

  7. Testimonials

    All 15 are quoted exactly as on your current site. Two mention durations that have since grown (“over the last 24 years”, “over 8 years”). Keep them as written, or would you like to ask the authors for an update?

  8. Credentials

    The preview uses “D.I.D., IDSA, SPE, SME” (your current site uses two different orders). Are your SPE silver and SME senior memberships still current?

Decisions for the new site

  1. News

    The last news post on the current site is from August 2016, so the preview leaves News out of the menu, where it would make Evolve look inactive. The most useful items (the DPN cover story, Design World, Canadian Plastics, the CES honoree) are kept as a dated list on About. Would you rather retire News, keep that list, or restart it with current projects?

  2. A contact form

    The preview links your email and phone directly and has no form. If you would like a form, it needs a service to deliver the messages (several simple ones exist) and a short privacy note.

  3. Domain, hosting and launch

    At launch the site moves to evolve-designsolutions.com: the images are copied off the old WordPress site, old page addresses are redirected to the new ones, and the site is opened to search engines. Who should we coordinate with for the domain and hosting?

Edits to confirm

Copy was tightened and typos fixed, but every claim comes from your current site. Counts that go stale (“over 30 years”) became dates (since 1984, founded 2005). Spelling follows Canadian use (colour, centre), with “mold” as the industry term. These 31 project titles differ from the current site; where a client name was part of the title, it now sits in the page’s client line instead.

Work / Defence and protective equipment

NVG Arm for Visor Shield

An adjustable night vision goggle (NVG) arm that mounts a ballistic visor shield to a special operations helmet system, for eye and face protection in unstable environments.

Designed for an international solutions provider specializing in protective equipment. The arm mounts to the helmet system’s NVG shroud and clips in directly, quickly and securely.

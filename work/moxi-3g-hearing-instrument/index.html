Moxi 3G Hearing Instrument

A wireless 312 CRT (canal receiver technology) hearing instrument that puts advanced technology into a small, discreet design.

The Moxi 3G carries the design language of the Swift, and gives hearing professionals fitting options for mild to moderately severe hearing loss.
